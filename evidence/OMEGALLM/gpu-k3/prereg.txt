Change under test: gr.om RMS kernels 10 (RMSF) and 11 (RMSB) run one warp per row instead of one
thread per row: lane l holds columns l + 32u (u < D/32) in registers, so every warp load/store is one
coalesced 128-byte line; RMSF reads v once (k2 read it twice), RMSB fuses its two passes and reads
v, g, da, dh once. Row sums are per-lane FFMA chains then a 16-8-4-2-1 SHFL.BFLY butterfly (grwred),
so results are NOT bit-identical to k2; every elementwise op keeps k2's order. gg.om launches
R*32 threads (arg 0 = R*32). grall refuses D or T not a multiple of 32. New build-time mutant r
(gr.om OMUTR 0): RMSB's row sum sum(da g v) skips the lane-1 butterfly step.
